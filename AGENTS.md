- An outline's sermon document (A2.1) is `sermon_outline_documents`.
  - **One way in.** It is read and written only by `src/features/sermon-editor/persistence/documents.ts`, behind `actions.ts` and the read route (`reader.ts`, A2.3B.2D), which `authorize("active")` and take the owner from the session.   - **What is stored.** Only what `restoreSermonDocument` accepted, as its `doc.toJSON()`. A refused document is never stored or repaired.
  - **Its own revision,** not `sermon_outlines.revision`. A save names the revision it was made against, and a stale one writes nothing.
  - **Size.** The limit is `documentLimits` (`persistence/model.ts`, 3 MiB since A2.3A) and nowhere else. It is never a CHECK, and an oversized document is refused, never cut. `serverActions.bodySizeLimit` (`next.config.ts`, 4 MB) must stay above it and under Vercel's 4.5 MB request cap; change the two together and keep `transport.test.ts` passing.
  - **Saving (A2.3A).** A save sends `prepareSave`'s plain copy, never the editor's JSON (its attributes have no prototype and the action encoder refuses them), and its answer is read with `saveOutcome` (`persistence/saving.ts`). The local sequence only names the snapshot; the document revision is the concurrency control.
  - **Strict JSON (A2.3A.1).** A document crosses into or out of persistence only through `toPlainJson` (`persistence/json.ts`), used by `prepareSave` and `prepareDocument`. It refuses, never changes, what JSON would alter (`not_json`) or `jsonb` cannot store (U+0000, unpaired surrogates: `unstorable_text`). Do not go back to `JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(…))`, and do not write a second copy. Content sent to the browser goes through it too, because React refuses null prototypes.
  - **Reading for reconciliation (A2.3A.1).** `readOutlineDocument` is read only: it never creates, converts, repairs, or writes. It answers another account's outline as `missing`, and never gives an unreadable document's content.
  - **Autosave (A2.3B.1)** is `persistence/autosave`: one controller per account, outline, and editor session (`createAutosaveController`), fed by `editorSource(editor)` from `onReady`; nothing else writes a session's draft. Keep its rules: one queue for every draft operation, one pending write (the newest), a snapshot claimed for a save only after its draft write settled (`claimSave`), a confirmation only of the claimed snapshot (`confirmSave`) that raises the watermark before anything is queued, and nothing reported kept before IndexedDB says so. It is mounted nowhere yet.
  - **Server saves (A2.3B.2A)** are `persistence/autosave/coordinator.ts`: one `createSaveCoordinator` per controller, given the authoritative server document as `base` (its revision must be the controller's) and a `DocumentTransport` (`transport.ts`); neither imports a Server Action (`documentTransport` takes the save action as an argument). Keep its rules: timers only (`serverSaveTiming`, 1.5 s idle, 10 s at most; never per keystroke, never polling), `flush()` before `claimSave()`, one request at a time, nothing sent while the controller has a `refusal` or that `canonicalDocument` refuses, a confirmation only of the sequence sent at exactly `expectedRevision + 1`, and nothing whose outcome is unknown sent again (`reconciling` holds the claim). Conflicts, refusals, and an unauthorized account stop it (`blocked`); a failure that wrote nothing is `failed`, retried within the bounded budget below. It never writes a draft itself. The canonical form the browser predicts is `canonicalDocument` (`persistence/canonical.ts`, where `prepareDocument` now lives); do not write a second one. It is mounted nowhere and is not production-ready.
  - **Reconciliation (A2.3B.2B.1)** is the coordinator's `reconcile()`. It reads the document once on entering `reconciling`, and again when called or by the bounded retries (A2.3B.2C), with one read at a time; it never polls and never writes. Keep its rules:
    - Compare only with `canonicalDocument` and `jsonEqual`, never by string or by revision alone, and against the `Submission`'s own `predicted` and `base` (never a later base).
    - Settle only at exactly R0 + 1 with what was sent, through `confirmSave`. Only a settlement the controller took is `saved`; the same evidence refused is `unsettled`, still held.
    - Release and block (`conflict`) only when the revision is past R0 or the document is gone.
    - Hold the claim whenever the request could still be written (revision R0 or lower, `unchanged` included, even when what was sent equals the base) and whenever the answer is not understood. An unauthorized read is `blocked` holding the claim.
    - A read is `reconciliation`, never a `confirmed` outcome.
    - Every `failed` from the server is `uncertain` (`saveOutcome`): its statement may have committed.
  - **The conditional retry (A2.3B.2B.2)** is the coordinator's one resend, sent by itself after a read finds `unchanged` (and through `retryUnchanged()`). Keep its rules:
    - Exactly the submission held: its content, sequence, and `expectedRevision`, under the same claim; never the editor's newer content, never a new submission.
    - Once per submission (`retried`, by identity), spent before anything is sent or told and never given back. Never add a timer, a loop, or a second resend here; the bounded retries (A2.3B.2C) only read, and nothing (`recover()` included) resets `retried`.
    - Only a confirmation at R0 + 1 settles it. A `stale` or refused answer to it is read back, never taken as a conflict; `missing` releases it; `unauthorized` blocks holding the claim.
    - Reads and the retry share one slot in flight (`inFlight`), set before anyone is told.
  - **Refusals (A2.3B.2B.3)** reach the draft only through the controller's `refuseSave` (queued after the snapshot's own write, accepted only for the claimed sequence); the coordinator never calls the store. Keep its rules:
    - Record only what is definite: `invalid`/`too_large` (server or the check before sending), a first attempt's `stale`/`missing`, or a read that releases. Never an uncertain answer or a retry's `stale` before its read.
    - Only a `refused` block is left, and only by a newer snapshot that is not the refused content; a conflict, `unauthorized`, an unresolved save, or `revision_mismatch` never is.
    - `refusalRecord` is metadata; never let a refusal's storage failure set `storageError` or change `autosaveStatus`.
    - An oversized snapshot is marked `too_large` in its own `writeDraft`, never over a `stale`/`missing` refusal. Whether a draft's refusal still applies is `currentRefusal` (`recovery/model.ts`).
    - A claim whose save may still be written is never released because its coordinator was disposed; a new coordinator on that controller is refused (`claimed`). No session ever takes one over (A2.3B.3): its draft is the only thing that crosses sessions.
  - **Network failures (A2.3B.2C)** are the coordinator's timeouts, bounded retries, and connectivity (`networkRecoveryTiming`, `autosave/model.ts`; `Connectivity`, `autosave/connectivity.ts`). Keep their rules:
    - A timeout (15 s a save or the retry, 10 s a read) is no answer: a save is `uncertain` and read back, never resent blindly; a read is `failed`. A late answer is dropped, never evidence. After `dispose()` nothing times out (a late confirmation still reaches the same controller).
    - One episode (`recovery`) at a time, one timer, tied to the held `Submission` or the failure. Automatic attempts only after a failed read, a read finding `unchanged` once the conditional retry is spent (the owner's decision), or a definite `failed` save; never after `diverged`, `unverifiable`, `unreadable`, or `unsettled`, and never from `blocked`.
    - Delays 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 s (at most 30 s), five attempts not counting the first, then `exhausted`. Only `recover()` after exhaustion starts a new cycle: never connectivity, a timer, `reconcile()`, `retry()`, or a listener. Never an unbounded loop, a heartbeat, or polling.
    - Connectivity is injected and advisory: offline holds back new requests (a due save `waiting`, the next attempt, a lost save's first read, uncounted) and cancels nothing; online makes one attempt at once, counted. The same news twice, or news while something is in flight, starts nothing. No `window` or `navigator` in the coordinator; A3 wires the signal.
  - **Recovery drafts (A2.3A)** are `persistence/recovery`, kind `documentDraft` in the offline `accountData` store, and nothing else reads or writes that kind. Every operation there is one transaction that compares sequences before writing: never let an older write replace a newer snapshot, or a confirmation of N remove N+1. A draft is removed only by a confirmation of exactly its snapshot, the person's discard, account deletion (`forgetAccount`), or `settleCleanups` after a restore's confirmed save (A2.3C, below), never by signing out, a setting, or a download. An unreadable draft is reported, never skipped, written over, or taken as "no unsaved work". A draft is never a save.

A save reads `sermon_outlines` only to find its sermon, locks that idea's row `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` before the document's (the order of `lockOutlineRows` and of a sermon's deletion; never the other way), and, when the stored content changed (jsonb inequality), calls `markDeveloped` in the same transaction (A3, the owner's decision).
