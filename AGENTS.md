- `ideas.last_developed_at` says when an idea's content was last worked on, and is null until that first happens; `updated_at` moves for any write and is not the same thing. - Outlines are `src/features/outlines` (`outlines.ts`, `entries.ts`) and three tables: `sermon_outlines`, `outline_entries`, `outline_entry_scripture_references`. Nothing else reads or writes them. Every query there filters by owner in its WHERE clause, and the tables' foreign keys carry the owner too; keep both. This rule governs `src/features/outlines`, its editor, and its three tables. The Preacher Studio editor experiment (PS-1A) is separate: it lives only in the development-only route `/lab/editor`, uses fixtures, persists nothing, and touches no outline table, no outline editor, and no sync queue. A document model for the application itself needs a later approved `PS-` phase (`docs/PREACHER-STUDIO-MIGRATION.md`).
- One outline to a sermon, made only by `createOutline`, which checks that the idea is a sermon with the idea's row locked (`FOR UPDATE`); `UNIQUE (sermon_id)` is what makes it one, so never drop it. Nothing in `src/features/outlines` writes to `ideas` except through `markDeveloped` (next rule): never a status, a format, a sermon type, or `archived_at`. Deleting a sermon deletes its outline (`ON DELETE CASCADE`); deleting an outline never touches the sermon. `getOutline` returns the outline of an archived or refiled sermon on purpose; `listOutlines` leaves those out.
- Meaningful outline work is developing the sermon (decided in Phase 3C). `markDeveloped` (`src/features/ideas/ideas.ts`) sets the sermon's `updated_at` and `last_developed_at` and nothing else, and is called only from `src/features/outlines/entries.ts`, inside the write's own transaction, when what the outline says really changed: words or passages that differ from what was stored, or an entry added, removed, or moved that bears content (it or something under it has a heading, notes, or a passage). Do not call it for an empty point, a move to the same place, a retry, anything stale or refused, creating or deleting an outline, numbering, folding, or opening a page, and do not write a second rule for what counts anywhere else.
- An entry is `introduction`, `point`, or `conclusion`, and a point is a point at any depth: never add a type, a depth column, or a stored path for subpoints, and never a limit on depth (`outlineLimits.entries` is the bound). The introduction and the conclusion are one of each, under nothing, over nothing, never moved, never numbered.
- Every write to an outline's entries begins with `lockForWrite`, which takes its locks in one order: the rows of `ideas` (the outline's sermon `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, and the point an entry is made from `FOR KEY SHARE`, lower ID first), then the outline's row, then its entries. Do not drop a lock, call `lockOutline` by itself, or take them in another order: a point's deletion and an entry made from it deadlocked when raced, and a sermon's deletion holds the idea before its outline, so a write that reached for the sermon after the outline would deadlock with it. A change of shape goes through `planInsert`, `planMove`, or `planRemove` (`tree.ts`) and `place`, which sets rows aside at negative positions before placing them; do not write `position` or `parent_id` another way, and do not add a CHECK that `position` is not negative.
- A number is never stored and never part of a title. Labels come only from `src/features/outlines/numbering.ts` (`numberOutline`, `entryLabel`), which has no server code and runs in the browser too. A built-in style is stored as its name; a custom one is validated by `numberingConfigSchema` and nothing else. A prefix, suffix, or separator is shown as text only, never as markup. Nothing in `tree.ts` or `numbering.ts` may call itself.
- The shape and the words are counted apart: `sermon_outlines.revision` for add, move, and remove; `outline_entries.revision` for a title, notes, or passages. Keep them apart, keep `expectedRevision` optional, and answer a stale change by writing nothing. An outline action answers with a `code`, the `revision`, and the entry; the editor is to go on from that answer.
- A saved point idea and an outline entry are two things. An entry made from a point (`sourcePointId`) is a copy of its title, notes, and passages, made by `createEntry` on the server, that remembers its source: never write back to the point, never update entries when a point changes, and never turn `sermon_points` into outline entries or require a row there. Deleting a point clears the link and keeps the entry; that is `ON DELETE SET NULL ("source_point_id")`, a column list written into migration `0013` by hand because Drizzle cannot express it. Never hand-edit `0013` again, and carry that clause into any later migration that touches the key.
- A passage on an entry is five numbers and no translation, as on an idea, validated with `src/features/scripture/reference.ts`, and written only by `setEntryReferences`. Never store verse text on an entry.
- The outline editor is `/outlines/<id>` (`OutlineEditor`). `/outlines/new?sermon=<id>` is where a sermon is given one, and never makes an outline by being opened. In the browser the outline has one owner, `createOutlineStore` (`src/features/outlines/editor/store.ts`): do not keep a second copy of the rows in component state, and do not call an outline action from a component except `deleteOutline` and `createOutline`.
