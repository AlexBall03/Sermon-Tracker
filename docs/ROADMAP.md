## Phase 2 — Core Idea Tracker

Search box, expandable filters with several values per filter and tags matched by any or all, active-filter chips, sort menu, card and list views with a remembered preference, numbered pages, a tag management dialog, tag selection in the idea editor, and returning to the same place in the library.     - **2C.1C: From the Bible to an idea** — implemented ([handoff](HANDOFF-2C.1C.md)). ## Bilingual milestone — English and Spanish (Phase 2C.2)

Implemented ([handoff](HANDOFF-2C.2.md)).

- Interface in English and Spanish, with a preferred language per account, and a Bible translation chosen independently of it
- A second Bible text, shown to people as **RVG** (the revision is RVG 2023, from the publisher's own file), beside the King James; switching translation in the reader
- Side-by-side reading in any pairing of the two, where the window is wide enough for two columns
- A switch an administrator can use to turn Spanish off for everyone, which deletes nothing
- What a person wrote (ideas, notes, labels) is never translated for them
- The owner reviewed the RVG's published terms before the text was added; the source and attribution are in `data/bible/README.md`
- Still in English: the public landing page, Clerk's own sign-in form, and the name, email, password, connected-account, and device forms inside Settings (Church & Ministry, added in Phase 2F.3, is in both languages)

## Phase 3 — Outline System

After Phase 2E and Phase 2F, in that order. In progress.

An outline holds the general shape of a sermon a person is thinking of preaching: short headings, brief notes, passages, and points under points. The sermon itself is written elsewhere. This is an outline organizer, not a place to write a manuscript.

- **3A: Outline foundation** — implemented ([handoff](HANDOFF-3A.md)). Three tables (`sermon_outlines`, `outline_entries`, `outline_entry_scripture_references`), each tied to its owner by the database; one outline to a sermon, made only for a sermon that exists; points nested to any depth, with an optional introduction and conclusion, one of each; ordering that is always whole, with a point moved together with everything under it and never under itself; numbering worked out when shown and never stored, in seven built-in styles and custom ones; an entry made from a saved point idea as a copy that remembers its source and survives the point's deletion; passages on an entry as numbers; stale changes detected, the shape and the words apart; owner-checked actions that say what each change left behind. Migration `0013`, applied to development only. No interface. Checked with type checking, lint and formatting on the files changed, its own unit and database tests with the existing ones it touches, a one-off run against the development database on concurrent connections, and a production build; the whole unit suite and the end-to-end suite were not run
- **3B: Structured outline editor** — next. The editor at phone and desktop widths; adding, editing, and removing entries; the introduction and conclusion; nesting, with drag and drop and controls that do the same without it; collapsing and expanding; autosave with a visible save state; choosing and customizing the numbering
- **3C: Sermon, library, and Bible integration.** An overview of outlines; making one from an existing or a new sermon; reaching it from a sermon's page and the library; placing saved point ideas in an outline; passages on an entry and reading them in place. The outline page joins the main navigation
- **3D: Preview, print, and hardening.** A preview, a printer-friendly layout, accessibility and narrow-screen refinements, and the release checks
